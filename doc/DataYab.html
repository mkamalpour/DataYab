7-Step Workflow

Complete in under 5 minutes
1

Load Data

Upload CSV, Excel, JSON, or Parquet. Auto-detects encoding and separators.

2

Auto Clean

One click — fix dtypes, remove constants, drop duplicates, fill missing, cap outliers.

3

Explore

Generate 7 chart types + auto-generated observations about your data.

4

Select Target & Split

Choose what to predict. Data split 80/20 for training/testing.

5

Train Model

Select model, adjust hyperparameters, enable SMOTE. Click Train — see metrics instantly.

6

Generate Insights

Get 15-20 business insights + health score (A-F) + actionable recommendations.

7

Predict

Manual dynamic form or batch CSV upload. Get predictions with confidence scores.
